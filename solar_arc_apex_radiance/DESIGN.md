## Brand & Style

This design system delivers a high-voltage, prestige e-sports and AAA RPG interface rendered in immaculate daylight conditions. Rather than retreating into stereotypical dark sci-fi tropes, it captures the clarity of high-tier competitive play and luxury anime games: radiant porcelain platforms, ice-silver chassis architecture, crystalline cyan telemetry, and vivid kinetic accents.

The aesthetic fuses crisp mechanical UI with fluid tournament telemetrics. It channels the angular dynamism of contemporary anime action titles, the exacting legibility of top-tier e-sports broadcasts, and the structural precision of technical HUD consoles. Surfaces feel like polished ceramic armor plates accented with holographic micro-grids, chamfered corner cuts, and luminescent conduits of energy.

### Core Visual Metaphors
- **Porcelain Chassis:** Pure white and pale ice-silver structural plates engineered to elevate tactical data without visual fatigue.
- **Kinetic Telemetry:** High-contrast electric cyan and saturated chromatic signals that function as responsive power conduits and active state indicators.
- **Architectural Edgecuts:** Tactical 45-degree chamfers and micro-notches that organize dense RPG statistics into instantly scannable combat modules.
- **Tournament Legibility:** High-contrast figures, monospaced battle metrics, and stark structural borders that maintain absolute readability in high-pressure play.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around luminous porcelain surfaces punctuated by concentrated chromatic energy bursts. The core canvas relies on pure whites, pristine silvers, and deep slate typography, allowing the vivid spectral spectrum to communicate status, elemental tiering, and operational states with authority.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#0284C7` Cyan Kinetic):** The baseline active conduit. Used for primary interactive triggers, active targeting brackets, tactical level-up gauges, and positive system states.
- **Secondary (`#7C3AED` Apex Violet):** High-tier resonance. Designates elite combat attributes, mystical or legendary item tiers, and secondary tactical actions.
- **Tertiary (`#D97706` Solar Amber):** Critical alerts, radiant currency, ultimate energy readiness, and prestige ratings.
- **Neutral (`#0F172A` Deep Carbon Obsidian):** Grounding neutral for high-impact typography, structural frame outlines, and tactical HUD separators.

### Environmental Tones
- **Porcelain Base:** `#FFFFFF` (pure surface plate), `#F4F7FB` (viewport field), `#E8EEF6` (sub-chassis panel), and `#DDE5F2` (recessed docking rails).
- **Status Accents:** `#059669` (Regenerative Emerald / Vitality), `#E11D48` (Critical Overdrive / Lethal Strike), and `#06B6D4` (Supercharged Ice-Blue).

## Typography

Typography functions as both structural framing and live data telemetry. The pair balances the mechanical geometry of Space Grotesk against the ergonomic, highly legible shapes of Manrope.

### Type Application Rules
- **Space Grotesk:** Reserved for headlines, operational callouts, tactical acronyms, and statistical counters. Numeric figures must utilize tabular lining figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to ensure zero jitter during animated battle counters and level progressions.
- **Manrope:** Drives all dialogue strings, item lore, quest descriptions, and system prompts. Set with balanced line heights to maintain rapid legibility across combat alerts and character management screens.
- **Casing & Spacing:** Tactical sub-headers, rank badges, and micro HUD data tags are permanently transformed to uppercase with expanded tracking (`0.08em` to `0.12em`), creating the feeling of calibrated military flight displays and premium tournament brackets.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model simulates a multi-tier tactical gaming console. It enforces a fluid 12-column architectural grid with disciplined perimeter boundaries that frame the screen like a cockpit visor.

### Grid & Responsiveness
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column grid, 2rem margins, 1.5rem gutters. Sidebars dock as ceramic wings flanking a primary dynamic viewport. Content containers anchor to structured aspect-ratio bounds to honor cinematic gameplay.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout, 1.5rem margins, 1rem gutters. Floating modules collapse into layered tabbed consoles with slide-in loadout drawers.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, 1rem margins, 0.75rem gutters. Edge-to-edge porcelain trays with thumb-accessible action clusters pinned along the lower third.

### Spacing Principles
- Density is tightly controlled: HUD counters, stat blocks, and equipment matrices use compact internal padding (`space-xs` and `space-sm`) to maximize visible game assets.
- Layout sections preserve generous breathing room (`space-lg` and `space-xl`) between distinct armor modules to prevent cognitive overload during fast-paced encounters.

## Elevation & Depth

Rather than relying on heavy murky drop shadows, the system establishes hierarchy through sharp ceramic layering, micro-step borders, and luminescent back-glows that simulate energized conduits.

### Structural Tiers
- **Surface Level 0 (Battlefield/Base Canvas):** Pale ice-tinted backdrop (`#F4F7FB`) accented with faint diagonal holographic raster grids at 3% opacity.
- **Surface Level 1 (Chassis Deck):** Solid porcelain (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px continuous precision hairline border (`#DDE5F2`).
- **Surface Level 2 (Target Modules & Cards):** Raised porcelain plates flanked by a 1px dual border: inner highlight `#FFFFFF` and outer structural edge `#CBD5E1`. Projects an ultra-diffused atmospheric aura (`0 8px 24px rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.08)`).
- **Surface Level 3 (Modal Displays & Ultimate Prompts):** Floating tactical overlays supported by a crisp 1.5px cyan kinetic boundary (`#0284C7`), casting a focused tournament glow (`0 12px 32px rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.18)`).

### Edge & Light Dynamics
Borders are active structural elements. Important cards feature an asymmetric highlight along the top or leading corner—a 2px accent strip in cyan or electric violet that reads as a light-catching beveled edge.

## Shapes

The shape grammar is mechanical, modern, and architectural. By adopting a tight roundedness profile (`0.25rem` / soft), the UI rejects overly bulbous mobile forms in favor of precise, tournament-spec instrumentation.

### Tactical Geometry
- **Precision Chamfers:** High-tier cards, primary action triggers, and active equipment slots employ clipped 45-degree corner bevels (4px to 8px chamfer cuts) on alternating opposite corners (top-right and bottom-left).
- **Notched Frame Inserts:** Status badges and numeric counters feature micro-step edge cutouts that evoke locking mechanical plates and modular data chips.
- **Energy Conduits:** Progress bars, XP gauges, and stamina trackers are built with angled terminal edges (slanted at 15 degrees) rather than blunt rounded pills, emphasizing velocity and directional momentum.

## Components

### Buttons & Action Triggers
- **Primary Tactical Trigger:** Porcelain face with an electric cyan border and vibrant cyan fill (`#0284C7`) featuring white Space Grotesk bold typography. The top-right corner is chamfered at 6px. Hover engages an energetic outer halo (`box-shadow: 0 0 16px rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.4)`) and an active internal micro-shimmer.
- **Secondary Armor Button:** Pure white background, 1px dark slate border (`#0F172A`), uppercase text with an accent diamond glyph on the leading edge. Transitions to an ice-blue fill (`#E8EEF6`) on press.
- **Ultimate Ability Trigger:** Electric violet fill (`#7C3AED`) wrapped in a gold amber hairline border (`#D97706`), vibrating with a calibrated breathing gradient overlay.

### Badges, Tags & Ranks
- **Combat Level Badges:** Angled parallelogram tags in solid deep slate (`#0F172A`) housing tabular golden amber numerals (`#D97706`).
- **Elemental Affinity Chips:** Ice-silver porcelain tabs (`#E8EEF6`) bearing a 4px glowing indicator gem on the left side (Cyan for Aero/Cryo, Violet for Astral, Emerald for Geo/Life, Crimson for Pyro).
- **Tournament Status Labels:** High-density Space Grotesk micro-labels with `0.12em` letter spacing, bracketed between mechanical corner crosshairs (`+ [ LIVE ] +`).

### Data Gauges & Progression
- **XP / Resonance Trackers:** Multi-segmented gauge styled with angled 15-degree dividers. Unfilled slots display as recessed slate (`#E2E8F0`); filled increments glow with a linear gradient from `#06B6D4` to `#0284C7`, crowned by a bright white leading particle head.
- **Dual Combat Meters (HP/Shield):** Primary health represented as an opaque emerald-to-cyan bar; interlocking shield values sit directly above as a crystalline ice-white translucent overlay with diagonal holographic striping.

### Cards & Equipment Chassis
- **Hero & Gear Cards:** Crisp white surfaces (`#FFFFFF`) with 1px border outlines (`#DDE5F2`). Hover cascades a cyan kinetic edge tracing around the perimeter. Top header displays a dedicated gear slot index (e.g., `SEC-04 // ATK`) in monospaced micro-type.
- **Equipped State:** Active equipment cards reveal a high-voltage glowing left rail (3px width) in violet or cyan, with an inset corner socket indicator.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Tactical Text Inputs:** Crisp white input troughs framed by `#CBD5E1`. When focused, borders snap to electric cyan (`#0284C7`) with a faint horizontal telemetry grid animating inside the container at 5% opacity. Monospaced input text in deep carbon (`#0F172A`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Sharp square indicators with 45-degree chamfered corners. Checked state flashes a radiant cyan core punctuated by a centered high-contrast geometric crosshair.

### Modals & Dialog HUDs
- Anchored with top and bottom telemetry rails displaying room coordinates, ping rates, and channel IDs. The modal chassis uses an ice-silver header band with an asymmetric angled close trigger (`✕`), maintaining constant visual connection to competitive broadcast overlays.
